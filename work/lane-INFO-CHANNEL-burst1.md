## Summary
- **8 ideas. 0 TRADE, 1 PROMISING (IC2), 4 CONDITIONAL / CONDITIONAL-research (IC1 flagship, IC3, IC5, IC6), 1 CONDITIONAL-barren-gate (IC4), 1 DECAY-BENCH (IC8), 1 cited-de-focused (IC7).**
- **The decisive number (IC1):** the observable-lock ratchet is DEAD on the live family as a *whole* — **29/29 market-locked rungs priced ≥98¢, 0 free-locks across 555 ladder-events / 9.0M OI.** Publicized counts (votes, SpaceX launches at 0.99/1.00, rate cuts) are efficiently tracked; the crowd is NOT slow on them. This is the number the Mesh lacked — it converts "COUNT family = live, build a monitor" into a **precise, mechanism-named gate**.
- **The gate (rescues, per taxonomy):** the +39.5¢ edge lives ONLY where the countable unit is **spoken words/topics on a broadcast**, settled on a **lagged official transcript** (LASTWORDCOUNT, KXFEDMENTION, KXPRESSBRIEFINGCOUNT) — verification is genuinely slow because no wire tallies spoken-word counts in real time. Numeric-event counts (votes/launches/cuts/awards) are wire-reported and priced.
- **The live shot (IC2):** KXFEDMENTION-26JUL is a scheduled, dated (Jul 29 presser → Jul 30 10am ET close, 18.5h window), OI-76k vehicle for the proven mechanism — the first *live* COUNT-ratchet vehicle since LASTWORDCOUNT went dormant. Discipline (from note 13): only buy YES sub-95¢ on words the tally shows were unambiguously said, ≥2-count safety margin; sell (NO) words priced >5¢ that were clearly not said.
