## THE SINGLE BEST LIVE VEHICLE (steer's ask) — A6c transition-timing, with dates
**Vehicle:** KXLLM1 weekly "Best AI this week" ladder (settles LMArena top-ranked provider, "Remove Style Control" toggle). Live cadence: closes every ~Mon 14:00Z — next closes **Jul 27, Aug 3, Aug 10, …** plus month-end (**Jul 31**) and **year-end (Dec 31)**.
**Thesis (concentrated, catalyst-driven):** the market efficiently prices a *static* leaderboard (incumbent 98-99¢) but the ONLY source of variance is a leadership transition — a new model topping LMArena. In the static regime there is no edge (proven: 11/11 favorite at 98.5¢). The concentrated bet is: **when a credible challenger model is scheduled/launched (GPT-5.x, Gemini 3, etc.), buy the challenger rung while it still trades cheap, before LMArena posts the new #1 and the book reprices.** This is Ackman (one asymmetric catalyst bet) ∩ INFO-CHANNEL (the public leaderboard update is the settle truth, watchable before the crowd reacts).
**Why it might pay:** KXLLM1 is a niche tech book, not a bot-farmed crypto ladder — the crowd may not watch LMArena continuously, so the flip→reprice attention gap could be minutes-to-hours (not the 1.9s crypto-book doctrine).
**Why it might not (honest):** (a) transitions are RARE — incumbent held 11+ weeks; (b) launches are public news, so the challenger may be bid up on hype *before* it's actually #1 (reflexivity/anticipation eats the entry); (c) 0 transitions in retained data = fully unproven.
**Cheapest decisive kill (capture-gated, ~2wk build):** `llmarena_capture.py` — poll the LMArena text leaderboard (Remove-Style-Control) + snapshot the live KXLLM1 ladder every ~15min. On the FIRST real #1 flip in-window, measure the lag between leaderboard-post-time and book-reprice, and whether the challenger rung was buyable below fair post-flip value. One flip decides the door.
