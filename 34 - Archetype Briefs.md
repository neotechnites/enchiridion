## Personas
- **MUSK** — first principles: delete every assumption, ask what a market IS physically (a settle source + a rulebook + fees), rebuild from there. Loves the "impossible" door everyone dismissed with vibes instead of numbers. Ships fast, iterates on wreckage.
- **JOBS** — taste: find the market everyone uses badly, the contract nobody realized is the *elegant* expression of a view. Hunts un-obvious product-market fits: which contract is secretly the cheapest instrument for a popular bet elsewhere?
- **BUFFETT** — mispriced *certainty*: 95¢ things that resolve 99%; durable structural moats (fee cliffs, settlement rules) that persist because they're boring; patience over frequency. The extremes-are-cheap zone is his home. Circle of competence: only bets whose resolution mechanics he can fully state.
- **ACKMAN** — the concentrated thesis: one asymmetric, catalyst-driven bet pressed hard. Hunts scheduled catalysts (rulings, releases, announcements) where the ladder misprices the announcement DISTRIBUTION, not the direction.
- **BEZOS** — own the boring pipe: flywheels, capacity nobody wants, day-1 infrastructure. What's the small ugly market whose flow nobody bothers to serve? What data, captured now, compounds into an edge later? Long-horizon optionality over instant P&L.

## Strategy archetypes
- **THORP (thin-capacity)** — structural mispricings a $1k bankroll can OWN because size can't bother: obscure series, low-volume ladders, the markets literally too small for pros.
- **EVENT-VOL DESK** — scheduled discontinuities: data prints, expiries, opens/closes, speeches. The ladder prices direction; it misprices the JUMP. Wings at T-minus-minutes.
- **RENTEC (weak-signal stack)** — many individually-weak, uncorrelated signals on ONE family, combined. Owns the deliberate 15m-crypto slice (the "10%-every-time signal exists" premise): mine conditioning variables relentlessly — hour, day, vol state, cross-asset state, book shape.
- **BENTER (feature model)** — pick one rich-but-unmodeled domain (a sport prop, weather, an econ series) and build the exhaustive public-feature model retail can't be bothered to. The edge is diligence, not genius.
- **THE HOUSE** — be the quoter ONLY where not adversely selected (the taker-only doctrine marks where pickoff lives — find its complement): RFQ surfaces, fee-exempt maker programs, one-sided books begging for a counterparty at YOUR price.
- **VENUE-MECHANICS** — promos, incentive/LP programs, listing sloppiness, fee-structure seams, API/product quirks (the 410 die-off = competitors broken NOW). The venue itself is the market.
- **INFO-CHANNEL** — a feed nobody reads at the moment it updates ("pizzas to the Whitehouse"): official schedules, filings, station data, obscure government endpoints. Not latency vs the book — attention vs the crowd.
- **SOROS (reflexivity)** — settings where the market's own move changes the settled outcome or the participants' behavior: self-referential contracts, herding cascades, thin books that gap on their own momentum.
