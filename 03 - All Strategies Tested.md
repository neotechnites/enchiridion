## ⚠️ Recent-30d only / regime-conditional (candidate model features, NOT standalone)
- **Buy NO when "up" is 20–60¢** (retail overpays for "up") — +2¢ recent, but vanished on up-days (−0.002 vs −0.017 down-days), didn't replicate on ETH. = bear-market artifact. *Regime-conditional.*
- **Buy the favorite in final ~2 min** (settlement-avg underpricing) — +1.5¢ up / +3.0¢ down recent, but up-side flipped to −2.8¢ in older period. Not durable as-is.
- **Near-strike late fade** (BTC within ~0.05% of strike, ~60s left) — +5–7¢ down-side recent, shrank to ~+1¢ gross / ~0 net & symmetric over longer history = regime. NOTE: at 60s-left, "near-strike" by 0.05% is already priced ~95¢ (60s too short for $30 to be a tossup); true coinflips are at tinier offsets or earlier in the window.
- **Combined multi-factor model** — OOS positive (+0.96¢, 61% win, 240 trades) but per-trade edge noisy and **offset feature may be lookahead-inflated** (1-min BTC bar peeks ≤60s ahead). Promising; needs clean rebuild → [[04 - Combined Model Plan]].

## ❌ Dead (don't revisit)
- **Directional / "first 5 min predicts"** — true but already priced; calibration error only ~2¢. The market is well-calibrated.
- **Buy favorites for high win-rate ("70 in a row")** — EV~0, and binary payoff = one loss wipes ~100 wins. Mathematically self-defeating.
- **Fade / mean-reversion scalp** — the −0.26 tick autocorr is **bid-ask bounce**; real fade **loses even at zero entry cost** (win ~24%). Moves are real, they stick.
- **Momentum / trend-follow** (BTC or contract) — BTC mean-reverts slightly at 1-min; trend-follow bleeds to fees (−3¢/trade).
- **Take-profit harvest** (buy, take +Nc, else hold) — 97% of positions tick up at some point, but net **negative**: the ~3% that don't go to zero (−30 to −62¢). Pennies in front of a steamroller.
- **1-minute time-boxed scalp** (cut if not profitable in 60s) — bounds loss but still negative; fees need ~4.5¢ move at mid, losers already −13¢ at 60s.
- **Sum-to-one arb** (hourly range buckets) — markets efficient (asks sum to 1.68–3.31, bids 0.5–0.84; $1 sits between). No riskless arb.
- **BTC/ETH pairs / convergence** — 0.89 correlated but spread is a random walk (51-hr half-life), reverts <50% in 15 min. Not cointegrated.
- **BTC→ETH odds signal** (trade ETH toward BTC's lean when odds gap) — win <50%, noise.

## Key cross-cutting lessons
1. **Binary 0/100 payoff + quadratic fee** is the disease that kills naked directional/scalp strategies. Escape it with: near-50¢ symmetric bets (streak), near-edge tiny-fee trades, or hedged structures.
2. **A bear month mechanically fakes "up overpriced."** Always regime-control (up-days vs down-days, up-weeks vs down-weeks, both BTC sides).
3. **Slice by actual BTC offset from strike**, not just contract price — price smears together offset and volatility.
4. **Tight spreads ≠ smart pricing.** MMs prevent arbitrage but don't make the price reflect streak/cross-crypto/regime info. That gap = the opportunity.
