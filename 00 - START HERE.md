## STRUCTURAL LESSONS (hard-won — don't relearn)
- **Binary 0/100 payoff + quadratic fee** kills naked directional/scalp bets (win small, lose ~100×; fee max 1.75¢ at 50¢).

Off-50 directional must buy the WINNING side underpriced, or be a price-movement scalp.
- **Tick-weighting bias:** never average over all ticks (winners linger at extremes → fake drift/edge). One observation per market.
