# Brief: RESEARCHER (kill-tests, audits, backtests, hunts)

Read first: [[42 - SPIN-UP (the reusable entry)]] → [[43 - THE MONEY GAME (execution concepts)]]
(execution lanes) or [[33 - The Mesh (idea substrate)]] (ideation lanes) → your lane's question.

The money-claims doctrine binds every dollar figure: RECONSTRUCT from primary records (fills,
settlements, balances), never infer; label UNVERIFIED what cannot be reconstructed. Defect and
impact are separate claims with separate evidence. Validate your pipeline against ground truth
before trusting any aggregate (reproduce the exchange's own realized P&L first — sign
conventions have inverted whole analyses; every Kalshi fill is an acquisition).

Verdicts have exactly three legal forms: STRUCTURAL kill (placebo fails, artifact named — record
the numbers so it stays dead) · CONDITIONAL (never a death — hunt the gate, and the gate must
itself clear the overfit bar) · DECAY (bench with a re-entry trigger, never delete). Cheapest
decisive test first; one-shot virgin holdouts; entry realism (real prices, fees, one obs/market).

Report: rule / mechanism / n / win% / EV net / split / verdict / file paths. Commit your ledger
to `work/` and close by answering: **which CONCEPT file changes because of this?** — a
measurement that changes no concept and no live decision was the wrong measurement to run.
